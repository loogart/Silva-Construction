Silva construction

New custom constructions

Are you looking for a general contractor in Outaouais that is trusted, well-known, innovative, and on the lookout for the latest market trends to build the home of your dreams? Don’t look any further!

At Silva Construction, you will find a renowned general contractor service. We have expertise in the construction of modern single-family homes, bungalows or two storeys, as well as high-quality multiplexes to meet the needs of the market.

For the past 12 years, Silva Construction has built a solid reputation with all its clients and suppliers. Our company supports you in the success of your construction project. Our know-how extends to design, interior and exterior construction, and renovation. Each of our creations is based on rigorous project management practices that ensure respect for expectations and deadlines.

Experts in the field

Silva Construction has positioned itself as a benchmark in the construction sector. Building many residential projects each year, the company boasts a remarkable know-how in the construction of numerous models of single-family homes, intergenerational homes, and multiplexes.

Concerned with offering you high-quality homes and carefree service, the homes built by Silva Construction are therefore guaranteed by the Garantie de construction résidentielle (GCR) for new homes.

Silva Construction has an efficient administrative team and a sales department that is attentive to the needs of its clients. When it comes to residential and commercial construction and major renovations, the SATISFACTION of our CLIENTS and the QUALITY of our products remain our top priorities!

Mission

Our primary goal is to build dream projects that meet the specific needs of our clients and fulfill their requirements through each step of their projects.

“We bring your dream to life!”

Vision

To become THE BUILDER of unique living environments for families and businesspeople in the greater national capital region by positioning ourselves as a leader in the construction field.

Values

Based on a number of values of great importance, Silva Construction realizes its vision and its mission with its clients. Our constant desire is to provide the best possible service, to continually seek to improve ourselves, to control every detail, and to be demanding toward ourselves and with our partner companies.
Professionalism and excellence

Offering quality services thanks to the know-how and experience of the entire team is the value that best defines Silva Construction.

Client satisfaction and attentiveness

Bringing a unique dream to life based on attentiveness toward our clients. Client satisfaction is at the heart of the decisions made by our management and site staff.

Cooperation

Our suppliers, subcontractors, and clients are partners with whom we deal in a spirit of cooperation.

Trust

The trust that you place in us is crucial. It is an important responsibility that drives us to bring you the best of ourselves in all the missions that you entrust to us, in respecting our commitments, in the quality of our service, and in our skills.

“Do you dream of having your home built but are still hesitating due to the significant cost that this can represent? Come see us and we will bring your dream project to life according to your budget.”
