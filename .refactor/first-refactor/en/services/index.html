Services

Fixed-rate turnkey

A comprehensive turnkey project concept, with Silva Construction model construction. This is our most complete service type: we take care of all the major stages of the planning and production of your home.

We ensure exceptional coordination between the resources and stakeholders, which allows us to stand out brilliantly, save time, and obtain optimal prices.

The price is fixed, and the cost of the work is firm. There are no surprises in the budget: this type of contract ensures peace of mind. It includes the labour, the products, and all the services required for a delivery according to plan.

Custom according to your personalized plan

Are you looking for a general contractor in Outaouais that is trusted, well-known, innovative, and on the lookout for the latest market trends to build the home of your dreams? Don’t look any further! Silva Construction will carry out the construction of your personalized custom plan.

Bring us your plan, and we will be able to evaluate the cost of construction for your project. Like our fixed-rate turnkey service, we ensure exceptional coordination between the resources and stakeholders, which allows us to stand out brilliantly, save time, and obtain optimal prices.

Rental

Have your children grown up and left the family nest, and now the house seems too big and maintenance is becoming a chore? Or are you a student or starting your career and don’t have the necessary funds to make a down payment on the purchase of a home? Rental is for you!

Silva Construction offers apartments for every taste and budget, focused on quality of life, without the hassles of ownership.

Renovations

Does your home need a fresh new look? Or are you opting for a kitchen, bathroom, or garage expansion that requires expertise and competence?

Choosing Silva Construction for your renovation project means opting for turnkey service where every detail is considered. Acting as a specialized contractor and project manager, we offer complete support for your renovation project. Our knowledge of the different materials available on the market makes us a perfect resource for your renovation projects. Regardless of your renovation project, we guarantee it in turnkey style.

“Building a home requires preparation. You have to think about the style of the house (small or large, wood or stone, the type of roof, the colour of the walls) and the plans in order to arrange the rooms and optimize the space—in short, you have to consider many things. To get a clearer picture, Silva Construction offers you ideas and advice to build the home of your dreams according to your budget!”
